Did You
See That?

A spot-the-difference game where every discovery changes what you see.

01

Introduce
the game.

Game description

Players compare two seemingly identical images and click the differences between them. Each correct discovery counts toward their goal, but the remaining differences immediately change. Across three increasingly difficult stages, players must look again instead of relying on what they noticed before.

Player experience

The game begins as a familiar observation puzzle, then introduces uncertainty. A difference the player was about to select may suddenly disappear. Through these shifting details, the game explores hallucination by creating doubt between what players remember seeing and what is visible now.
