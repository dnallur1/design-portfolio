01 / VISUAL STORYTELLING

Winter Bear.

A quiet winter landscape.
A feeling of being loved and remembered.

FINAL COVER / Winter Bear by V — an academic cover reinterpretation

THE INTENT

Warmth in a
quiet season.

Translate the tenderness, nostalgia, and reassurance of V’s “Winter Bear” into a square album cover. The image holds companionship within a cold landscape, using two bears as its emotional anchor.

My role
Concept development, prompt direction, reference selection, and visual refinement
Approach
AI-assisted image generation and iterative editing
Design language
Sage green, dusty mauve, soft film grain, diffused light

THE PROCESS

Building the feeling.

Three selected stages
from the image development.

THE FINAL DECISIONS

Let companionship
carry the story.

The final refinement adds two bears beside the bench, lightens the snowfall, and brings out the green. Their shared gaze gives the landscape a human feeling: comfort, memory, and closeness.
