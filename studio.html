02 / SPATIAL DESIGN

3D Studio.

A future workspace where designers,
clients, and AI meet.

FINAL DESIGN / Overall studio view

THE INTENT

Make ideas
inhabitable.

Imagine an interior design studio where AI becomes part of the physical design process. Workstations, material exploration, and an immersive presentation room support designing, experimenting, and collaborating with clients.

My role
Spatial concept, prompt direction, material refinement, and camera walkthrough direction
Tools & approach
Blender, AI-assisted reference imagery, and iterative visual review
Priorities
Clear activity zones, private work, client collaboration, and human design judgment

REFERENCE DEVELOPMENT

A room within a studio.
